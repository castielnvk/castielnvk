# O Əsrdə — brand

Azerbaijani Instagram/TikTok series: the viewer wakes up in another century and lives one day
through someone else's eyes (a ruler, a baker, an oil worker). First-person, everyday detail, the
time of day shown at the top of the screen as the day goes on.

`brend-baxisi.html` compares four directions (published preview:

## Chosen direction: 2 · Saat (26 Nov 2026)

The user picked direction 2 out of: 1 Göz açılışı, 2 Saat, 3 Arxiv, 4 Gündəlik həyat.

**Idea:** the brand is the clock. The coloured time box next to the wordmark is the same box as
the on-screen clock, and every hour has its own colour.

- **Wordmark:** `O ƏSRDƏ` in Big Shoulders Display 900 (caps), followed by a time stamp box
  (`06:00`) in Big Shoulders 800 on the current hour colour, aligned to the cap top.
- **Symbol / profile picture:** a tall condensed "O" ring (ink) on the hour-colour disc, with an
  hour hand from the centre. The "O" is a 24-hour dial with 00 at the top, so 06:00 points right
  and each hour turns the hand 15°. The profile picture uses the 06:00 version.
- **On-screen clock:** full-width hour-colour band at the top with large `HH:MM` digits in Big
  Shoulders 900 (ink), the small symbol on the right, and a 24-segment day bar under the band
  (past hours white, current hour filling in the hour colour).
- **Reel cover:** hour band with wordmark + `TƏBRİZ · 1501`-style place/year in JetBrains Mono,
  day bar, footage, then an ink panel with the title in caps (middle line in the hour colour).
- **Opening frame:** hour band with the time, day bar, footage, opening line bottom-left in Big
  Shoulders 800 over a dark gradient.
- **Time change motion:** minutes count up, the hour digit rolls up, the band colour blends to the
  next hour, the day-bar segment fills, the symbol's hand turns 15°.
